Uzo FC Defeat Henry FC, Become Table Leaders

Uzo FC handed Henry FC their first defeat as the match ended 2-1 in their favour.

The winners were quick to show intent in the first minute when Kelechi Osuoha dribbled past two opponents only to shoot over the bar in a first scare. Two minutes later Uzo FC would almost score with Chijoke Ogazi's free-kick while the ball missed the bar by a whisker's width.

Henry FC were not left out of the highlights as they would also cause a scare when , in the 8th minute , Chibuzor Ogbuji received a fine cross off Chibuike Nweke's boot only to shoot directly at the goal-keeper who handled well the shot. Four minutes after, Henry FC would have a chance again to lead as Chibuike Nweke will give a through-ball to Marvelous, but lost it as Marvelous blasted the ball over the bar.

Twenty-three minutes in the first half Henry FC would miss another chance to lead with Chibuike Nweke doing well to beat the Opponents' goalie, after a head pass from Marvellous, only to be denied by the goal frame. The same Chibuike would supply a cross to Chibuzor Ogbuji in the 33rd minute who would wastefully shoot over the bar.

Two minutes in the 2nd half Uzo's Humble Anele headed the ball towards the net from a corner but Jude Ononiwu did well to parry the ball over the bar. However 8 minutes after Henry FC succumbed to Uzo FC's pressure as Kelechi Osuoha made a run to reach a fine pass from Ogazi Chijoke to slot home the first goal of the match.

Henry FC fought back fiercely and in the 65th minute their effort would pay with a goal from Wisdom Samuel who had a free-kick-borne ball, from Chibuzor Opara , fall at his fore, after a poor clearance, to equalize the scoreline.

Both teams kept fighting for a second until the 97th minute when Uzo FC would snatch the winner. Uzo FC's Mbadiwe Vitalis took advantage of an erroneous play from Daniel Chukuwunonye, after Henry FC took a corner kick in the opponents half , ran with the ball and struck home the winner. The match ended 2-1 to take Uzo FC to the top of the log and 3 points in the bag.

Micheal FC Secure First Win of the OUL26.

Michael Anoruo FC got their first and the first 3 points of the OUL 2026 tournament as they overcame Emeka Ike FC in tough battle last Saturday.

Micheal FC scored their opener and the only goal of the match 12 minuites from Kickoff when Somto Oparaugo, reached for the pass given by Esomchi Anele to hit a low shot, getting the better of Emeka's goalie, as the ball cruised into the leftside of the inner-net.

Emeka FC tried to get one back with Junior Osuji having a shot at the target but Onana denied him as he parried rightwards for corner 10 mins later

Michael FC did not relent as they to had their fair share of chances for a second goal. Most notably , in the 30th minute when Chiemerie Osuji,who has been terrorizing his opponents' defence with skillful dribbles, got a cross from Wisdom Ogazi and stroke the ball only to be denied by legs Emmanuel Akunna, the opponents goalie. Emmanuel would also parry a fierce shot, rightwards, from a free-kick by Chibundu Munachi at the final minutes of the game.

Despite several attempts by Emeka FC , team Michael stood firm and ensured that the match ended in a 1-0 victory for them. This win sees Michael FC sit at the top of the log.

Emeka FC vs Henry FC ends in a 2-all Thriller as 13 year-Old Sochima makes OUL Debut

Emeka FC and Henry FC held each other in a 4-goal thriller sharing two goals apiece as the OUL 26 kicked off.

The opening match had both teams taking the whole of the first half sizing-up each other with little sparks and a barren outcome.

However 8 mins into the second half Emeka FC drew first blood with a powerful shot from Ibe Michael from far left- one that left the Henry FC's Ononiwu helpless as watched the flying ball land into his opposite end of the net.

Henry FC pressed for a reply and their hardwork payed in the 68th min after the captain of the opponents Ebuka Ezema handled a goal-bound header resulting into a penalty which Chibuike Opara converted with ease to balance the score-line

Both teams pushed on for another goal until Emeka FC took the lead again after Ekeh Udochukwu got a low cross from Domike Uzochukwu and hit home a second in the 86th min. Henry FC responsed two mins later with a counter-attack led by Nweke Chibuike whose through-pass found the captain of the side, Opara Chibuike, for another beautiful come-back and a brace from a strike beating Emeka FC's Onana to his right.

The spectators were cheered higher as 13-year old Sochima Osuoha was introduced into his first OUL game after 77 mins of play. Sochima displayed confidence and flair in his runs and passes to the delighy of the spectators. Will he become the Youngest individual awardee? We leave the answer to time.
